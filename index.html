How It Works

1. Initiate Transaction

Create a transaction and and give us the details of the counterparty, we verify the identity and send the transaction request to them for review .

2. Make Payment

Once payment is made, the funds are held securely in Vesicash, Until the previously agreed terms of the transaction are met .

3. Release Funds

Once the terms are met and both parties are satisfied, the funds are released by Vesicash.
