Who We are?

With online fraud valued at $15bn annually, and payment fraud increasing by 53% year on year, our mission is to eliminate not just peer-to-peer fraud but all payment fraud so that everyone everywhere can transact with each other in total confidence.

As an innovative leader in the Online Financial Technology, we believe in providing; a secure, simple,affordable and reliable digital payment services that enables individuals and businesses conduct transaction without the fear of being defrauded.

Our proprietary payment process creates a unique environment of trust and security that every other peer-to-peer payment system lacks.
